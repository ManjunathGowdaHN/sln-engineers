02 / SERVICES

OUR SERVICES

Electrical expertise,
end to end.

Our experienced electrical team offers the following services across residential, commercial and industrial requirements.

01

Electrical Design & Consultation

Comprehensive electrical design, load calculation & estimation, panel and wiring designing.

02

HT & LT Works

HT & LT yard construction, cabling and cable termination, earthing systems and lightning protection systems.

03

ESCOM & CEIG Liaisoning

ESCOM approvals, drawing approvals from CEIG (Electrical Inspectorate), DG and lift approvals.

04

UPS & Generator

Emergency lighting and UPS installation & servicing, cable fault detection and jointing.

05

Panel Board & Transformer

Distribution and metering panel commissioning, transformer commissioning, maintenance and oil filtration.

06

Internal Electrification

Residential, commercial & industrial projects, professional electrical installations and Electrical Annual Maintenance (AMC).
